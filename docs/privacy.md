# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: June 2026**

This privacy policy describes how our application collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our Atlassian Jira integration.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you authorize our application via Atlassian OAuth, we collect your profile information (such as name and email address) and Atlassian site URLs. When you interact with the application, we process Jira issue data necessary to fulfill your requests (such as creating, reading, updating, or commenting on issues).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide the integration's core functionality—allowing you to manage Jira issues via our platform. We do not sell your personal information or Jira data to third parties.

## 3. Data Retention and Security

Your OAuth tokens are stored securely to maintain your session. You can revoke access at any time from your Atlassian account settings, which will immediately prevent our application from accessing your Jira data.

## 4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact our support team.
